07 / CHECK THE TREASURE

Is it really buried treasure?

Found something promising? Check it before you use it.
1
WHO MADE IT?
Can you tell who wrote it?
Look for a name and where it was published. If you can’t tell, be cautious.
2
HOW DO THEY KNOW?
Do they show evidence?
Look for facts, interviews, data, or links to sources. If there’s no evidence, don’t take the claim at face value.
3
DOES IT HELP?
Does it answer your question?
Check that it covers your topic and is recent enough. If it doesn’t help answer your question, keep looking.
If you’re unsure, compare what another reliable source says.
